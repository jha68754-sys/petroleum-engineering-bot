# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Enterprise Petroleum AI Platform

## 1. Product Scope
The platform provides an autonomous cognitive engineering assistant for upstream petroleum disciplines including Reservoir, Production, Well Testing, Artificial Lift, PVT, and Diagnostics.

---

## 2. Functional Requirements
- **Automated Intent Detection**: Automatically identify engineering discipline from natural language or structured inputs.
- **Missing Data Collection**: Intelligent identification and prompting for missing engineering parameters.
- **Rigorous Calculations**: Calculation engines grounded in verified SPE and textbook equations.
- **Explainable AI & Recommendations**: Actionable engineering recommendations accompanied by physical rationale, equations, and literature references.
